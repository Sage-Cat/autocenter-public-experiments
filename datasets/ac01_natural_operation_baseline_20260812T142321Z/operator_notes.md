# Public operator notes

- The sensor and collector remained in one fixed operational placement for the
  autonomous run.
- The existing Wi-Fi network was not disabled, retuned, or otherwise modified;
  it is part of the natural interference environment.
- Customers and staff could use the area normally, but their presence and
  actions were not logged as experimental labels.
- No deliberate fault injections or controlled human-action blocks belong to
  this run.
- The collector completed automatically after 86,400 seconds. One brief serial
  disconnect was recorded and recovered automatically.
- Exact building address, photographs, SSID, BSSID, board serial, device path,
  host name, and network addresses are outside the public bundle.

The absence of an operator-event timeline is intentional and must remain part
of the scientific interpretation: clock-hour variation is observational, not
human-presence ground truth.
